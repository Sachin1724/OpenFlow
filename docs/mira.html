What MIRA looks like

MIRA appears in the capsule while you dictate Odia: a flat face that watches your cursor, blinks, and breathes with your voice.

Move your pointer: MIRA follows it.

Recorded from the app. English keeps the plain indicator. Switch to Odia and MIRA appears; speak Sambalpuri and the badge changes mid-sentence; let go and it refines, then pastes.

How MIRA works

One neural model hears the speech. Everything after it is fixed rules, so MIRA cannot invent words. Times measured on 40 real recordings and 1,800 sentence runs.

  1. 110ms

    Hear

    AI4Bharat's IndicConformer turns 16 kHz audio into Odia letters. Median time per recording across 40 recordings averaging 7.6 seconds, word-model search included.

  2. 16paths

    Pick the words

    Real words win over near misses (କାମ, work, over କମ): a beam search keeps the 16 likeliest readings and scores them with a word model of 400,000 Odia words and 3.3 million word pairs.

  3. 0.019ms

    Find the dialect

    Marker words name the dialect: Sambalpuri, Ganjami, Kalahandia, Anugulia, Baleswari, or standard Odia.

  4. 0.027ms

    Write standard Odia

    Regional verbs and pronouns become standard written Odia; slurred word joins are repaired, and the sentence ends with ।, ? or ! from its tone.

  5. 0.053ms

    Clean up

    Hesitations go, spoken corrections keep only the correction, and amounts become numbers, so ପାଞ୍ଚ ଶହ ଟଙ୍କା (five hundred rupees) is written ₹500 in the final text.

  6. 225,270

    Keep English in English

    English said inside Odia is matched against a table of 225,270 Odia spellings of English words, and written back in English letters.

Conditions and limits

What these numbers do and do not show.

  • One machine. Every number comes from one laptop CPU with int8 weights. A graphics card or a slower CPU will give different times.
  • Read speech. FLEURS is people reading sentences aloud. Fast conversation and noisy rooms score worse.
  • Two dates. FLEURS was measured on 31 October 2026. Size, memory, the 17× speed and the step times were measured on 11 November 2026 on 40 OdiaTTS recordings.
  • Rules have edges. Dialect rules cover the forms in the tables; a regional word they don't list stays as spoken. Numbers cover 1 to 20 and the round tens.
  • Odia or English. Auto mode calls a sentence English when at least 40% of its words come back in English. An Odia sentence full of English nouns can be misread.
  • Repeatable. The benchmark code and data sources are on the research page.
