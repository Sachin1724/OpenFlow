New in version 1.1.0

  • On-device NPU acceleration.

    Windows 11 Copilot+ PCs (Intel Core Ultra AI Boost) now run Whisper models on the NPU at under 2 Watts. Dictate and record meetings all day with zero fan spin and virtually no battery drain.

  • Apple-grade capsule HUD.

    Re-architected dark HUD material with fluid iOS toggle switches, inset grouped cards, and macOS-style popovers with leading accent checks and system typography.

  • Silent capsule-only boot.

    OpenFlow launches directly into the subtle floating dynamic island capsule without opening the heavy dashboard window. The full dashboard opens on demand from the tray or capsule.

  • Press-to-record hotkey box.

    Set custom hotkeys by simply clicking and pressing your keys. Automatically pauses global listeners during recording to prevent shortcut capture collisions.

  • Deterministic model downloads.

    Zero unsolicited background downloads. OpenFlow strictly downloads only the exact variant you select, and transparently substitutes cached local models.
