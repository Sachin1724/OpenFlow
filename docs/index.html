What it does

Hold a key and talk , and your words are typed into any app , in 99 languages, on your own graphics card . Nothing uploaded. Nothing capped.

No subscription, no word counter, no cloud. The model downloads once and works without internet after that.

How it fits into your day

To begin, run the installer. A short setup asks for your language and downloads the speech model once, about 74 MB for English.

Within a minute, you are dictating. Hold Ctrl and Shift anywhere, speak, and let go. The text is pasted into the window you were in.

After that, it stays out of the way. OpenFlow lives in the tray, starts when you sign in, and lets go of the GPU when you have been quiet for ten minutes.

From then on, it is just a faster keyboard. Every dictation is saved in a searchable history, and settings are one tab away.

To choose by hand

  1. Open the dashboard and go to Settings.
  2. Set Run on to CPU, GPU or Automatic.
  3. Set Speech model to Tiny, Base, Small or Medium.
  4. Press Save. The new model loads in the background.

Have an NVIDIA card but OpenFlow says CUDA is not usable? The GPU libraries are missing. Install them once, then restart OpenFlow.

pip install nvidia-cublas-cu12 nvidia-cudnn-cu12
