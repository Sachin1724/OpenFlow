New in 1.3.2.

Out now. Download the latest installer and everything below is included.

  • Near-field voice isolation.

    In a room with other people talking? OpenFlow automatically isolates whoever is closest to the microphone using physics-based inverse-square calibration and suppresses background chatter without needing voice profiles.

  • Instant dictation start.

    GPU kernels are pre-warmed in the background right after model loading. No more 1–3 second warm-up stall on your first spoken word.

  • A Dynamic Island capsule.

    Pick it in Settings. It rests at the top of the screen, springs open for dictation, types your words in underneath as you speak, and queues every notice.

  • A lighter, steadier app.

    The speech engine runs in its own background process and unloads during idle. A stuck model can never freeze the UI, and an automatic watchdog restarts OpenFlow if needed.

  • Silence stays silent.

    Recordings with no speech never hit the model, and repeated-phrase loops are cut before transcription.

  • OpenFlow for Android (beta).

    Private, on-device mobile voice typing is here for Android devices, with an offline Odia speech model and floating capsule.

How it works

  1. Hold Ctrl+Shift and speak OdiaSilero voice detection trims the silence. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. IndicConformer hears OdiaAI4Bharat's 120M-parameter Odia model on ONNX Runtime, quantized to int8: 197 MB, about a third of a second per sentence.
  3. IndicTrans2 translates, if you chose EnglishAI4Bharat's distilled 200M translation model on CTranslate2, int8: about 100 to 400 ms per sentence.
  4. Pasted into the app you are inWord, WhatsApp, a browser form or a government portal, with your clipboard put back.

Models by AI4Bharat: OpenFlow Odia ASR (CC-BY-4.0) and IndicTrans2 (MIT).

How Odia got a voice.

  1. The problem we kept hearing

    About 3.7 crore people speak Odia, yet almost nobody types it. Panchayat clerks, teachers and doctors write it in English letters or not at all. Every voice tool that could help was cloud-only, English-first or paid, and the one everyone uses, Whisper, has no Odia at all.

  2. The idea

    OpenFlow already turned speech into text in any Windows app, offline. What if Odia worked the same way: hold a key, speak Odia, and real Odia script appears, or English for the forms that demand it, without the voice ever leaving the laptop?

  3. How we built it

    We measured first. Then we added AI4Bharat's IndicConformer on ONNX Runtime and squeezed it from 493 MB to 197 MB with int8, losing 0.3 points of accuracy. IndicTrans2 on CTranslate2 handles English, and a small Qwen2.5 model rewrites on request. No new heavy runtime: everything rides on libraries OpenFlow already shipped.

  4. Where it is now

    On 100 real FLEURS recordings OpenFlow writes Odia with a 21.7% word error rate, against 136.5% for Whisper small, 100% in Odia script, in 283 ms a clip on a laptop CPU. Spoken Odia becomes English at chrF 60.1, against 20.8 for Whisper.

Measured, not claimed.

We ran every engine OpenFlow has on the same real Odia recordings, on an ordinary laptop CPU, and scored the output against human transcripts.
