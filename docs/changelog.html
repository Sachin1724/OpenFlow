OpenFlow version 1.3.0

Coming next

Not released yet. It ships soon, and the installer will appear on the releases page.

New

  • A Dynamic Island style capsule. It is a black shape at the top or bottom edge of your screen that rests small, tucks away when idle, springs open to listen and write, shows alerts, and opens a settings panel when you click it.
  • Width and height controls for the capsule and the island, with an option to grow on screens wider than 1920 px.
  • A live typing preview. Your words type in steadily under the capsule while you speak.
  • A new Apple-style dashboard with a sidebar, grouped settings and a rebuilt Model Library.
  • The meeting notes viewer now matches the new look, and the sidebar shows the bare logo mark.

Better

  • The speech engine now runs in its own process and is released after 10 idle minutes, so the interface stays responsive and the memory comes back.
  • Intel NPU detection no longer loads extra drivers on PCs that are not Intel.
  • If the app ever stops responding, it restarts itself and writes a freeze log.

Fixed

  • The app no longer freezes after idle time or sleep, when the tray, taskbar and Start menu stopped responding.
  • Silence or room noise no longer produces invented text or looping phrases.
  • Exported meeting notes keep one paragraph per sentence instead of one block.
  • Scrolling the settings page no longer changes the sliders and drop-downs under the cursor.
  • The live preview no longer re-flickers every few seconds.
  • Only one capsule can show at a time.

The new dashboard. Capsule settings: style, opacity, width and height.

OpenFlow version 1.2.0

30 October 2026

Better

  • If the app stops responding for 20 seconds, it saves a freeze log and restarts itself.
  • The speech model stays loaded on the CPU and NPU, and is only released from the GPU after one hour idle (it was 10 minutes), so the next press is quick.

Fixed

  • The tray, taskbar and Start menu stopped responding after a while, because unloading the model, refreshing the microphone and closing the audio stream could block the interface. They now run in the background.

OpenFlow version 1.1.9

30 October 2026

New

  • Install with npm: npx github:Sachin1724/OpenFlow downloads and runs the latest installer.

Better

  • Automatic mode now picks the Intel NPU on a first run on Core Ultra PCs, instead of staying on the CPU, and logs why when no NPU is visible.
  • A GPU that froze stays off: OpenFlow remembers it and starts on the NPU or CPU next time instead of stalling for 10 seconds again.

Fixed

  • The hotkey works again after 10 idle minutes. The next press used to touch the capsule from the wrong thread and froze dictation.
  • The landing page demo no longer runs off the right edge on phones.

OpenFlow version 1.1.8

29 October 2026

Better

  • Fewer Odia mistakes: an Odia word list (400,000 words, 12.7 MB, fetched once in the background) lowers test word errors from 21.1% to 20.1% and character errors from 5.8% to 5.5%.
  • Odia output is limited to Odia script, so stray Bengali or Devanagari letters no longer appear.

Fixed

  • Odia always uses the Odia model. With Whisper small selected, picking Odia used to run Whisper anyway, which wrote Bengali or romanised gibberish. The Odia model is now downloaded if it is needed.

OpenFlow version 1.1.7

29 October 2026

New

  • A capsule opacity slider in Settings, from 30% to 100%, applied live.

Better

  • A tap shorter than 0.35 seconds, or a Ctrl shortcut, is ignored instead of starting a recording that never ended. Hands-free dictation is the Toggle mode in Settings.
  • The dashboard no longer opens at boot. The installer stops adding a second Startup shortcut and removes the old one.

Fixed

  • Dictation pastes again. Ctrl+V sent as one batch was silently dropped on some Windows builds, so the keys are now sent one by one.
  • No more endless spinner. A live preview call on the GPU could freeze and block every dictation after it. OpenFlow now gives up on a stuck call after 10 seconds and finishes on the CPU.

OpenFlow version 1.1.6

New

  • Meeting notes and in-place voice edits with a built-in local writing model. Select text, give a command, and it is rewritten on your PC.

OpenFlow version 1.1.5

28 October 2026

Better

  • Dictation on laptops without an NVIDIA GPU is more than 65% faster, using simple greedy decoding and pinning work to physical CPU cores.

OpenFlow version 1.1.0

28 October 2026

New

  • Intel NPU acceleration on Windows 11 Copilot+ PCs, under 2 watts, so all-day dictation and meeting transcription cause no fan spin and almost no battery drain. It falls back to NVIDIA CUDA or the CPU automatically.
  • A redesigned capsule HUD with grouped cards, fluid iOS-style switches and macOS-style menus with a check mark on the current choice.
  • A silent, capsule-first start. OpenFlow boots straight into the small capsule, and the dashboard opens from the tray or the capsule.
  • A press-to-record hotkey box in Settings. Global hotkeys pause while you record a new one.

Better

  • OpenFlow downloads only the model you choose, and a cached variant such as base.en counts as base, so nothing downloads behind your back.

The capsule HUD.

OpenFlow version 1.0.0

28 October 2026

New

  • A single-file Windows installer of 94.7 MB with Start Menu and Desktop shortcuts. It installs to your user folder and needs no administrator rights.
  • Live dictation on the GPU, with under 150 ms latency on NVIDIA RTX cards and automatic fallback to the CPU.
  • A floating capsule that never steals focus from your active window, with a live audio meter.
  • Autonomous meeting mode for Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams and Cisco Webex. It writes structured notes (agenda, discussion, decisions, action items) and keeps them on your PC.
  • Instant microphone hotplug: USB and Bluetooth mics are noticed the moment they connect or disconnect.
  • Five tone presets: Smart Clean, Developer, Email and Formal, Bullet Points, and Verbatim.
  • More than 99 languages with automatic detection, and prompts that keep Hindi and Odia in their own scripts.

Meeting mode, live captions.
